Kiboar is a **mixed-licence collection**, and only because of its symbols.

- Every 3D model and every footprint is **MIT**, and so are the symbols drawn
  here. 133 models and 131 of the 146 symbols carry the authorship and the
  licence in the file itself.
- A further 11 models come from the Keyswitch KiCad Library and are used under
  its **MIT** option, so they are MIT too.
- Fifteen imported symbols keep the licence of their source.

`NOTICE` in the repository root lists every third-party origin, the affected
files and the evidence.

## Before using it commercially

- Ten symbols are SnapEDA imports and five are Component Search Engine imports,
  both under **CC BY-SA 4.0** with the Design Exception, so they need
  attribution when the files themselves are redistributed.
- The switch, hotswap, cutout and stabilizer models are from the Keyswitch KiCad
  Library, dual **MIT / CC BY-SA 4.0**, and this repository uses them under the
  MIT option; `LICENSE-kiswitch` in the repository root carries the notice.

No model or footprint carries a non-commercial or NoDerivatives licence. The
third-party 3D bodies were removed rather than kept under terms that would block
commercial use, so the footprints ship with their pads, courtyard and fab
outline and no body.
